This is Sourced, a research agent that answers questions with evidence you can verify. Inference runs on Nebius Token Factory, using the model shown here. Tavily provides real-time web search.

Let's ask who won the twenty twenty-six Nobel Prize in Physics, and for what work. The agent plans complementary queries, searches the web, and checks which parts of the question the evidence covers.
