- **What the work showed:** Kagan discovered a way of manipulating chemical reactions that allows a greater excess of one mirror image ("enantiomer") to be created than previously assumed possible, while Soai demonstrated autocatalysis — a process in which one product of the reaction acts as its own catalyst — enabling one mirror image to be generated "from nothing" [3][4].
- **Why it matters:** The discoveries address how homochirality (the presence of only one mirror-image form, as in amino acids in living organisms) can emerge spontaneously — a puzzle described as over a century old [2][6].
